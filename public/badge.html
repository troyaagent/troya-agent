    02 / What it checks
    
    
    
      
    Four states, one line
    
      
    A 0.01 ETH buy is routed and simulated against the pool as it is, then sold back in a test, with transfer tax and fees measured. A thin young curve is reported, never counted as a failure on its own.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    01
    Passed
    Route, simulation, test sell, tax and fees all went through at that moment.
    
      
    02
    Caution
    Something worth reading first, such as fees above 5%. The page says which one.
    
      
    03
    Failed
    A check failed: the swap would not go through, a sell back reverted, or the tax is high. The page names it.
    
      
    04
    Not tested yet
    The curve holds no buyers' ETH, so there is nothing to test a sell against. It updates once people buy.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    03 / What it does not say
    
    
    
      
    Read it plainly
    
      
    A badge is a snapshot, not an audit or an endorsement, and a coin can change after a check. Troya is not paid for badges and does not list or rank coins with them. Not financial advice.
