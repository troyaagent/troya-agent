Capabilities

Everything it reads. Every way it acts.

Troya splits its work in two. The reading half looks at markets, pools and contracts and can never move a coin. The acting half drafts, checks and sends orders, and only inside the walls you set. Both halves are open to you in the app, over MCP and through REST.

02 / Guarded execution

Acting takes three locked steps

No single call can jump from an idea to a filled order. Each step hands a token to the next, and every step reads your limits again before it lets anything through.

simulate_tradeSimulate

Walks the live Hyperliquid order book to estimate fill price and slippage. Swaps get the same from quote_swap. Still read-only.

prepare_orderPrepare

Runs Troya Gate against your policy, then returns a one-time confirmationId tied to the exact parameters.

execute_intentExecute

Accepts nothing but the confirmationId, checks the policy once more, then submits. Over MCP it stays in dry-run until you turn that off.

01

In the app

The assistant writes the order as a proposal card. You review it and sign in your own wallet, or the Agent Wallet signs it when you have switched it on and the order fits its caps.

02

Over MCP

The same three steps exposed as tools for Claude Desktop, Cursor or Claude Code. Dry-run is the default, so a fresh install cannot place a live order by accident.

03

Over REST

Public GET routes for reading. Execution routes need an x-api-key and an Idempotency-Key, so a retried request cannot fire twice.

05 / Always on

On watch, on record

Sentry checks every new launch, Autopilot and the morning brief work from your own rules, and every action the agent takes lands in your journal.

Troya Sentry

Every new Pons launch is checked by Troya Gate about 90 seconds in, and again at 5, 15 and 60 minutes while it is quiet. New Uniswap v3 and v4 pools against ETH, WETH or USDG, and new pools on the other Robinhood Chain launchpads, go through the same checks. The Telegram channel posts only the clean passes with real buying: at least 0.5 ETH on the curve (or in the pool) and 25 or more independent buyers. Wallets that buy every new coin, such as bots and farms, are not counted. Names with look-alike letters are left out, and so is a coin reusing a ticker posted in the last 24 hours, unless it has more than twice the independent buyers of the first one (that post then names the other coin). Failed and caution results stay in the record (every result as JSON). A Trade in Telegram button sits on every post.
