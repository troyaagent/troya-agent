How the score works

Weights, caps and what it never says

Each part gives a value from 0 to 1, weighted as below. The score is the weighted share of the parts that could be read, then the lowest cap that applies. A part that could not be read says so and is left out; nothing is filled in.

Caps: one serious finding keeps the score low however good the rest looks.

    Bands: 75 to 100 No major flags, 50 to 74 Some flags, 25 to 49 Risky, 0 to 24 Red flags. Tokenized stocks and majors (WETH, USDG, cbBTC) are named as such and not scored like launches.

    Automated checks, not an audit. A score is a snapshot of what Troya read from the chain at that moment, never a safe stamp, and a coin can change after a check.
