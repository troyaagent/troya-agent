03 / Stay safe

What we never do

Scammers copy every growing project. These rules make them easy to spot.

We never DM first

Nobody from Troya opens a private chat with you: not an admin, not “support”, not the dev. A DM that starts the conversation is not us.

No one asks for your keys

No seed phrase, private key, password or “wallet verification” link, ever. Troya never sees your main wallet's keys; you sign every transaction in your own wallet.

No paid listings or promo by DM

Offers of exchange listings, trending spots, votes, boosts, KOL calls or “marketing packages” in DMs or replies are not from Troya, and the bot removes them in the group. Real announcements come from the links below.

Check the contract

Many coins copy the $TROYA ticker. The only $TROYA on Robinhood Chain is 0x1FA81ddaAAc83240803B3F2d20595E40Edc1a074. Use the Buy button on this site: it opens Troya Swap with that address and the Gate checks it first.

Look-alike names

Impostors use names like “Troya Support” or "Troya Admin" and swap letters in handles. Admins carry the admin tag in the group; anyone else using the name is not the team.

Too good is a signal

Guaranteed returns, airdrops that need a deposit, “claim” sites and urgent countdowns are scams. Troya never promises prices or profit.
