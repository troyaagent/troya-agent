$TROYA flywheel

The flywheel

Creator fees on $TROYA buy $TROYA back, burn it, and lock liquidity in the pool where nobody can remove it. Every cycle is signed by the creator wallet, and every number on this page is read back from the chain.

01 / One cycle

Four signatures

The creator wallet signs each step in its own browser wallet. Troya builds the transactions and checks them; it holds no key and moves nothing.

01

Claim

The creator's share of every $TROYA trade (70% of the 1% Pons fee plus the 1.9% creator tax) is credited to the creator wallet in the Pons fee escrow. The cycle claims it.

02

Buy back

Part of the ETH buys $TROYA in its pool, through Troya Gate first: route, price impact, a simulated swap and a test sell.

03

Burn

The bought $TROYA is burned with the token's own burn(): the total supply goes down, on chain, for good.

04

Lock liquidity

The rest goes into the $TROYA pool as a full-range Uniswap v4 position minted straight to 0x…dEaD. No one holds that NFT, so no one can ever take the liquidity out.
