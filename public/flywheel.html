$TROYA flywheel

The flywheel

A flywheel cycle takes a share of the creator fees on $TROYA, buys $TROYA back, burns part of it and locks the rest as liquidity nobody can remove. The burn campaign of 17 Nov 2026 bought back and burned $70 of $TROYA; a second one, from 18 to 19 Nov, bought $2 every 2 hours (12 buys, $24) and burned each buy in full, and was done on 19 Nov. The next campaign will be announced on X and Telegram before it starts. Every cycle and every number on this page is read back from the chain.

01 / One cycle

Four signatures

Each step is signed by the creator wallet: by hand in its own browser wallet, or by the automatic mode, which keeps the creator key encrypted on the server and can sign only these four calls. Troya builds and checks every transaction, and each one is read back from the chain.

01

Claim

The creator's share of every $TROYA trade (70% of the 1% Pons fee plus the 1.9% creator tax) is credited to the creator wallet in the Pons fee escrow. The cycle claims it.

02

Buy back

Part of the ETH buys $TROYA in its pool, through Troya Gate first: route, price impact, a simulated swap and a test sell.

03

Burn

Part of the bought $TROYA is burned with the token's own burn(): the total supply goes down, on chain, for good.

04

Lock liquidity

The rest goes into the $TROYA pool as a full-range Uniswap v4 position minted straight to 0x…dEaD. No one holds that NFT, so no one can ever take the liquidity out.
