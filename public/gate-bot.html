Gate in Telegram

Put the Gate in your group

Add @TrojanTroya_bot to any Telegram group. Anyone there can send /gate 0x… with a Robinhood Chain coin and get Troya's read in the chat: the verdict first, then what it is based on. No admin rights needed, nothing to set up.

Add the Gate to your group

01 / What /gate says

The verdict, then the why

One reply per coin, in plain words. A clean contract never hides a bad launch: if the code looks clean but the launch ring is red, the reply says exactly that.

01

Verdict

The first line: no red flags in these checks, caution, red flag or high risk, with the reason in one sentence.

02

Bytecode

A score out of 100 for the contract code only: mint, pause, blacklist, fee setters, upgradeable proxy, and whether the owner is renounced.

03

Launch ring

For Pons coins, from Ring Radar: how many wallets were freed from the snipe tax at launch, how much of the supply the dev and those wallets took in the first blocks, and a clear, amber or red call.

04

The Assay

Who holds the coin: what the dev holds and sold, the top 10 wallets, the first buyers, and any red or amber flags.

Each reply links to the full check in Troya, with a test sell. Automated checks, not an audit, and never a safe stamp.

02 / Add it

Three taps

The bot posts one short hello when it joins, so your members know what it does, and then speaks only when someone uses a command, mentions it or replies to it.

01

Add the bot

Tap Add the Gate to your group, or open @TrojanTroya_bot, send /start and tap the same button. Telegram asks which group.

02

Pick the group

An ordinary member is enough. The bot does not need admin rights and does not ask for them.

03

/gate 0x…

Paste the coin's contract address after the command. If your group has other bots with a /gate command, use /gate@TrojanTroya_bot 0x….

03 / Commands

For members and admins

Coin replies carry Buy and chart buttons (Troya Swap through the Gate, Fomo, the chart, DexScreener). Your group's admins decide whether they show.

01

/gate 0x…

The full read on one coin, for anyone in the group.

02

/ring · /assay

Only the launch ring, or only The Assay, for a closer look. /price 0x… gives the live price.

03

/gatebuttons off

Admins only: coin replies in your group come as text only, with no Buy or chart buttons.

04

/gatebuttons on

Admins only: brings the buttons back. /gatebuttons alone shows the current setting.

04 / What it never does

A guest, not a moderator

The bot is an AI agent and says so when asked. It is a guest in your group: it reads coins, it does not run your chat.

01

No trades in groups

Trading and launching only happen in a private chat with the bot, from your own Agent Wallet, and only after you tap Confirm.

02

No keys, ever

It never asks anyone for a seed phrase, a private key or a password, and never DMs first. Anyone who does is not Troya.

03

No moderation

No join checks, mutes, bans or deleted messages in your group. Those tools stay in Troya's own community.

04

Little kept

For your group it keeps the chat's ID and name, the button setting and when it said hello. Your chat is not logged; only a /bug or /idea a member sends reaches the Troya team.
