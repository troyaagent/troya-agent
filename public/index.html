Capabilities

Robinhood Chain Desk

Talk to it the way you would talk to a trader sitting next to you. Troya pulls the Robinhood Chain tape, Pons curves, Uniswap pools and Hyperliquid perps into one desk, then hands you a ticket that is ready to sign.

Ask & Quote

Point at a token, a pool or a perp and ask. Troya answers with live numbers (price, depth, funding, your balances) plus a contract risk read, in whatever language you typed.

Learn more

Robinhood Chain Swaps Live on Robinhood Chain

Orders for coins and tokenized stocks route through Pons curves and Uniswap v3 and v4 pools on chain 4663. Pools that are too thin, too new or misbehaving are skipped, and the quote is refreshed once more before your wallet opens.

Perps Ticket

Any Hyperliquid perp, either side, with leverage, take-profit and stop-loss set in the same ticket.

Agent Wallet

Agent Wallet

Want it hands-free? Spin up a small Agent Wallet and Troya can act from chat (swaps, transfers, perps) without a popup every time, and never past the daily caps you set. It is custodial: the operator keeps its key encrypted, so fund it like pocket money. Your own wallet stays out of reach.

Autopilot & Playbooks

Write a playbook once (a stop-loss guard, a take-profit ladder, a weekly DCA) and the server checks it every 20 seconds. Autopilot is opt-in and only runs while your tab is open.

Troya Gate

Nothing moves until the Gate clears it: route, price impact, pool depth, any fee or creator tax, a simulation of the exact transaction and a test sell. Whatever happens next is written to your journal.

Principles

High walls. Your rules at the gate.

Real Markets

Mainnet only: Robinhood Chain and Hyperliquid, with real money. Each action passes a check before it runs, an operator kill switch can freeze everything, and MCP execution ships in dry-run.

Risk First

Order size, leverage, a market allowlist and a daily USD ceiling are tested before every action. The Agent Wallet lives inside them, and Pause freezes it on the spot.

Full Audit Trail

Every move is logged with the reasoning behind it, and the whole journal is readable inside the app.
