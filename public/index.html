Inside the walls

The story is old. A gift at the gate, and something inside it.

On Robinhood Chain, every new coin is a gift at the gate. Troya opens it before you do, and an agent you control works from the inside. Talk to it like a trader sitting next to you: it pulls the Robinhood Chain tape, Pons curves and every launchpad, Uniswap pools, Base, Arbitrum and Solana routes, and Hyperliquid perps and spot into one desk, then hands you a ticket that is ready to sign.

  1. I Troya Gate

    Every coin, opened at the gate

    Before a swap is shown, and again before the Agent Wallet signs, Troya Gate reads seven things: route, price impact, depth, fees, a simulation, a test sell and transfer tax. Fail one, no order. It runs again when you press Confirm, and whatever happens next is written to your journal.

    Gate a coin

  2. II Telegram

    A trading desk in every chat

    Paste a coin in a private chat with @TrojanTroya_bot, or tap Trade in Telegram on a Sentry post. /long, /short, /close or plain words like "long ETH $20 at 3x". The Gate runs first, Buy only appears when it passes, and nothing goes through until you tap Confirm. It trades from your Agent Wallet, inside your caps.

    Open @TrojanTroya_bot

  3. III Troya Sentry

    The night watch never sleeps

    Every new Pons launch goes through the Gate about 90 seconds in, and again at 5, 15 and 60 minutes, and so do new pools on Uniswap and the other Robinhood Chain launchpads. Only clean passes with real buying reach the channel: at least 0.5 ETH on the curve (or in the pool) and 25 or more independent buyers. Farm wallets and copies are left out. Checks, never an endorsement.

    Follow t.me/troyasentry

  4. IV Sentry Autopilot

    Your rules, around the clock

    Give the agent your rules, like "launches that pass the Gate above 1 ETH raised, 0.01 ETH each, take profit at 2x, stop at -30%". Start in practice with play money at real prices, go live from your Agent Wallet when you are ready. The best results show on a leaderboard you can copy from.

    Try it in practice

Also in the desk

Everything else, one click in

Ask and quote

Point at a token, a pool or a perp and ask. Live numbers (price, depth, funding, your balances) plus a contract risk read, in whatever language you typed. The chat remembers the conversation, and New chat keeps old ones in an archive you can reopen.

Ask Troya

Swaps

Coins and tokenized stocks route through Pons curves and Uniswap v3 and v4 pools, and coins from the other Robinhood Chain launchpads (Long.xyz, Bankr, Frontier.fun, Abyss, Virtuals and more) through KyberSwap. Thin, new or misbehaving pools are skipped, and the quote is refreshed once more before your wallet opens.

Swap

More chains

The same Swap page also trades on Base and Arbitrum (routes from KyberSwap) and on Solana (routes from Jupiter, with Phantom, Solflare or Backpack). Pick the network, and the Gate runs on that chain before anything is signed: route, depth, fees, a simulation and a test sell.

Pick a network

Perps ticket

Any Hyperliquid perp, either side, with leverage, take-profit and stop-loss in the same ticket. Funding takes one transaction: ETH on Robinhood Chain becomes USDC on your own Hyperliquid account.

Spot on Hyperliquid

Buy and sell HYPE, UBTC, UETH and the other Hyperliquid spot coins from your own Hyperliquid account, with the same trade-only key as Perps. A Spot Gate reads the live book first, and orders never fill more than 1% from the price.

Agent Wallet

A small wallet Troya can act from without a popup every time, never past the daily caps you set. Withdrawals only go back to your own wallet. It is custodial, so fund it like pocket money.

Agent Wallet

Autopilot and playbooks

Write a playbook once (a stop-loss guard, a take-profit ladder, a weekly DCA) and the server checks it every 20 seconds, tab open or not. Without the Agent Wallet it leaves you a ticket to approve.

Playbooks

Daily AI brief

Every morning: your positions, PnL, liquidation distance, funding, the nearest stop, the market and the day's Sentry, with a two-line summary. Smart alerts when a stop is close.
