02 / The post

Write it like this

The short way@Troyaagent launch Wooden Horse $HORSE

That alone is a clean launch: 0% creator tax and no initial buy. Add tax 2% for a creator tax (0–4%, all yours) and buy 0.01 for an initial buy in ETH, only if you want them.

@Troyaagent launch Wooden Horse $HORSE tax 2% buy 0.01
Website and Telegram (optional)@Troyaagent launch Wooden Horse $HORSE site: woodenhorse.xyz tg: t.me/woodenhorse

Written into the coin with it, for good, so every site shows them. site: takes any https address, tg: a t.me link or your group's @name.

In your own words (English)Hey @Troyaagent, please create a token called Moon Cat, ticker MCAT, 2% tax and buy 0.01 ETH

Troya reads it and checks that the name, the ticker and every number are really in your post. If it is unsure, it replies with the format instead of guessing.

The image

Attach it to the same post, PNG or JPEG. Troya crops it to its centre square (up to 1024 px), since every site shows coin logos in a square or circle; the web form and Telegram do the same. GIFs turn into video on X and don't work. No image? Post without one: the coin then has none, for good.

Name and ticker

Written into the coin forever. Any name and ticker you like: up to 48 characters for the name, letters and numbers for the ticker (16 at most), saved exactly as you write it: $horse stays horse on X, in Telegram and on troya.codes.

04 / Creator fees

Where the money goes

Launching

The Pons launch fee (0.0005 ETH today) comes from your Agent Wallet, which needs 0.0006 ETH for a launch without a buy. Troya pays the gas. Troya charges nothing to launch.

Your creator fee

Holders of 1M $TROYA: 100% yours, to your own wallet (the one linked to X): 70% of the 1% trading fee, so 0.7% of every trade's volume, plus your whole creator tax, before and after the coin graduates. Everyone else: 0.6% of volume plus the whole tax; Troya takes 0.1% of volume.

Claiming it

Creator fees wait on the coin's Pons curve and in the Pons fee escrow. Claim them in one click on Troya's Creator desk with the wallet you launch with: it shows what is waiting for each coin and sends it to your wallet.

Your initial buy

The coins land in your Agent Wallet. See Selling your initial buy below.

Troya’s 0.1% always goes through a fee-split contract that nobody can change, on every launch path.

05 / Selling your initial buy

Two ways to sell it

Your initial buy sits in your Agent Wallet, not in MetaMask. Every sale runs through Troya Gate and your Agent Wallet caps, and the ETH lands back in the Agent Wallet, which can only withdraw to your own wallet.

On troya.codes (easiest)

Open Launch from X, find the coin under Your launches and press Sell 25%, Sell 50% or Sell 100%. Check the review, confirm, done.

In Telegram

Link Telegram once in the app (Settings → Telegram alerts), open a private chat with the Troya bot and paste the coin's address. Pick Sell 25% / 50% / 100%, then Confirm.

Then move the ETH home: Agent → Wallet → Withdraw sends it to your own wallet only. You can also export the Agent Wallet key to use it in another wallet app.

06 / Rules

Limits that keep it clean

  • Launch on X is open to everyone. Without 1,000,000 $TROYA: one launch from X a day. Holders: no daily limit.
  • Without 1M $TROYA: at most 3 launches a day across X, Telegram and troya.codes (UTC days).
  • Creator tax from 0% to 4%, fixed for good.
  • Initial buy up to 1 ETH, and within your Agent Wallet caps (by default $100 a trade and $250 a day; you can change them).
  • One coin per post: the same post is never launched twice.
  • A launch is final: name, ticker, image, links and tax can't be changed.
  • Names and tickers are free: any name up to 48 characters, any ticker of letters and numbers up to 16, saved exactly as typed. The image is optional on X and in Telegram, and required on troya.codes.

09 / FAQ

If something looks off

Troya didn't reply to my post.

Check five things: the post tags @Troyaagent and is a new post from your linked X account; the account is public; Launch from X is switched on; without 1M $TROYA, it's your first launch from X today; your Agent Wallet has enough ETH. Whenever Troya reads a launch post and can't launch it, it replies with what to fix.

How do I sell my initial buy?

Launch from X → Your launches → Sell 25%, 50% or 100%. Or paste the coin address in a private chat with the Troya bot, or ask Troya in the app. Every sale goes through the Gate; a coin with a thin curve can still be sold, you only get a warning.

How long does it take?

Usually seconds: Troya sees the post as it is published, launches, makes your initial buy after the first 3 seconds, then replies.

Can I launch from another X account?

No. Only posts from the X account linked to your wallet launch coins for it.

Do I need an image?

Not on X or in Telegram (there, reply /skip), but a coin launched without one has no image, for good. On troya.codes an image is required. PNG or JPEG; any shape is cropped to its centre square.

Can I change the name, ticker, image, links or tax later?

No. They are written into the coin when it is launched.

Is the Gate result a safety stamp?

No. It is a live check of the coin at that moment, not a promise about what happens next.

I don't hold 1M $TROYA. Can I still launch?

Yes: one launch from X a day and up to three a day in all, from X, Telegram or troya.codes with your own wallet. You keep 0.6% of volume plus your whole creator tax, and Troya takes 0.1%. Hold 1M for no daily limit and the whole creator fee. You can also launch from Telegram: send /launch to the Troya bot.
