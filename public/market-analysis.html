01 / Read only

Analysis lives on its own floor

No order buttons here

Analysis tools cannot place, sign or cancel anything. Execution sits one tier up, behind your policy and an expiring confirmation, and it has to be asked for separately.

Same answers everywhere

The assistant in the app, the MCP server and the REST API call the same tools, so a number you see in chat matches the one your script reads.

03 / Token and social

Check before you hold

New coins launch on Robinhood Chain all the time, on Pons Family and elsewhere. A few quick checks catch the obvious traps before you put ETH into one.

01

Token health

analyze_token_health reads the contract's bytecode for mint, pause, blacklist, fee-switch and owner powers, and tells you which ones it found. It is a heuristic; a clean result is not a guarantee.

02

Social

get_social_info searches X for a token or ticker. It needs an X API bearer token configured by the operator, and every post it returns is fenced as untrusted text.

03

Discovery

get_new_pairs lists the newest pools and Pons launches on Robinhood Chain with price, liquidity and volume. Troya Sentry checks every new Pons launch about 90 seconds in and posts the clean passes with real buying to a public channel.

04 / Data sources

Live data, limits stated

Each number comes from somewhere specific. Here is where, and what each source cannot tell you.

Hyperliquid

Perp prices, funding, open interest, candles and order books from Hyperliquid's public info API. Perp orders go to Hyperliquid mainnet.

Candles are only as fine as Hyperliquid serves them; very new listings have short histories.

Robinhood Chain

Balances, Pons curve and pool state, and quotes read directly from chain 4663 over RPC. Gas is paid in ETH, and every transaction can be checked on robinhoodchain.blockscout.com.

A chain read is a snapshot. Prices can move before you sign, which is why a quote is refreshed once more before your wallet opens.

GeckoTerminal

Token lists, launch times, prices and volume for Robinhood Chain launches and pools: Pons, Uniswap and the other launchpads and DEXes, such as Long.xyz, Bankr, Frontier.fun, Abyss, Goo, Clank.trade, o1 and Virtuals.

Tokens GeckoTerminal has not picked up yet will be missing, and some listed tokens have no route Troya can swap through yet (Pons, Uniswap v3 and v4, or KyberSwap).

KyberSwap

Routes for Robinhood Chain coins that Pons and Uniswap cannot reach, such as the other launchpads above, and for swaps on Base and Arbitrum. Troya builds each swap from a fresh route, sends it only to KyberSwap's known router, and Troya Gate simulates it with a test sell first.

A route is only as good as the pools behind it at that moment; thin pools give high price impact, which the Gate shows before you sign.

Jupiter

Solana swap routes across pump.fun and the other Solana launchpads and DEXes, built for your own address and signed in your own Solana wallet. The Solana Gate reads mint and freeze authority and Token-2022 extensions and simulates the exact swap first.

Fresh Solana coins can lose their route or liquidity within minutes; a quote is a snapshot.

LI.FI bridge routes

Routes for moving ETH into and out of Robinhood Chain from Arbitrum, Base and Ethereum.

Most routes land in minutes, but the time and the cost depend on the route available when you send.

Robinhood Chain prices are quoted against the pair token, usually ETH or USDG. USD figures are converted at the current ETH price and are approximate. Tokenized stock prices come from their on-chain pools. Market caps are estimated from total supply, not circulating supply.
