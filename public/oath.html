Safety · Oath

Oath kept, or Oath broken

A dev signs a public pledge for a Pons coin: how much of the supply the team sells per day at most, how long creator fees stay unclaimed, and which wallets it controls. Troya holds the dev to it in three tiers, all live: watched in public, held by Troya's rules, and locked in code in the Oath Vault, a contract proven with Halmos and tested with real coins (Verify, step 32).

02 / Three tiers

Watched, held, locked

Every Oath is watched from the moment it is signed, and Troya's rules apply to it on their own. The Oath Vault is for devs who want the limit enforced by a contract instead of by Troya.

TIER 1 · WATCHED

In public

Every ten minutes Troya reads what the dev and the wallets it named sold into the Pons curve or the pool that UTC day, and any creator-fee claim during the lock. Each Oath shows as kept or broken, with the transaction that broke it.

TIER 2 · TROYA'S RULES

With a cost

A broken Oath turns the coin's Gate verdict and its Sentry post to caution, and the wallets behind it cannot launch through Troya for 30 days.

TIER 3 · THE OATH VAULT

In code

The dev locks coins in the TroyaOathVault contract: nothing leaves before a cliff, then at most a set amount a day, and only the wallet they are locked for can take them out. It has no owner and no admin. Five of its properties are proven for every input with Halmos, and it passed a real-money test on 19 Nov 2026.

03 / Take the Oath

For devs: sign it with the launch wallet

Connect the coin's Pons deployer or creator-fee wallet. Signing costs nothing and sends nothing; it is an EIP-712 message (domain "Troya Oath" v1, chain 4663). Once taken, an Oath cannot be edited or removed.
