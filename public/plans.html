Good to know

Plain answers

01

What does a trade cost?

You pay the network's gas in ETH and the venue's own fees: the pool or curve fee on Robinhood Chain, Base, Arbitrum or Solana (gas there is ETH or SOL), Hyperliquid's trading fee on perps and spot. Troya adds nothing to a trade. Troya's own fees are two: 0.1% of trading volume on coins launched through Troya by wallets holding less than 1,000,000 $TROYA, taken from the creator's share and never added for traders, and 0.01 USDG per Gate API check after the free ones.

02

What counts as a message?

Each question you send the assistant uses one message from your wallet's daily allowance. Reading the market pages, swapping and trading by hand do not use any. The app shows how many you have left.

03

Is the Agent Wallet part of Free?

Yes, when the operator has it switched on. It is optional, custodial and meant for small amounts. You can run the whole desk with your own wallet instead.

04

Will Free stay free?

The beta is free. If that ever changes, it will be announced here first, and nothing you already hold or set up will be locked behind a paywall without notice.
