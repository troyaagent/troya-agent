01 / The burn

1,000,000,000 minus totalSupply()

$TROYA was minted once, 1,000,000,000 coins straight into its Pons curve. Every burn since used the token's own burn(), which takes the coins out of the total supply instead of parking them at a dead address. So the whole burn is one read on the token contract: 1,000,000,000 minus totalSupply() now.

$TROYA burned…reading totalSupply() on chain

Reading totalSupply() on chain. The live number is also at /api/proof/burn.

Read it from the chain yourself sends eth_call totalSupply() and balanceOf(0x…dEaD) to Robinhood Chain through this site's RPC relay and shows the raw answer


      
Or send it to the public RPC yourself: POST https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com with {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_call","params":[{"to":"0x1FA81ddaAAc83240803B3F2d20595E40Edc1a074","data":"0x18160ddd"},"latest"]}. The answer is the supply in wei (18 decimals).

05 / Team and accountability

    

      
Who signs what

      
Troya is built in public by the Troya Agent team. These are the accounts it speaks from and the wallets it answers for, each one already named on this site; no person is named here.

    

    

      
01
Code in the open
The app, the Gate, the Agent Wallet caps, Sentry, the bots and the contracts are public under MIT, with the commit history.
GitHub organisation troya-agent repository npm

      
02
Where the team answers
Announcements on X and in the Telegram group, where bug reports and questions get answers; Sentry posts in its own channel.
X @Troyaagent Telegram group Sentry channel Report a bug

      
03
No admin keys on staking
TroyaStake has no owner, admin, pause, upgrade, fee or rescue function: owner() reverts, and only a staker can move their own coins. The Holder Pass and the Oath Vault have no owner either.
TroyaStake source Read on chain

      
04
The one owner key
The fee-split factory has an owner, the team wallet. It can change only where Troya's share of a launched coin's fees goes; it cannot change a coin's split or touch the creator's share.
TroyaFeeSplitter source Factory

      
05
Custody, plainly
Trades on the Swap page are signed in your own wallet. The optional Agent Wallet is custodial: the operator stores its key encrypted, it pays back only to its owner, and it should hold pocket money only. The Arena's and the flywheel's keys sit on the server the same way.
Risk controls Safety

      
06
No audit yet
There is no third-party security audit of Troya. Sixteen properties of three contracts (the fee splitter, the Holder Pass and the Oath Vault) were proven with a prover and are listed on Seal; that is not an audit.
Seal and proven properties
