V1

Shipped15 Nov 2026

Sentry and the Gate badge

The first roadmap, built the day $TROYA launched: a watchman for every new coin, trading from Telegram, and perks for holders.

  1. F1Troya Sentry OpenEvery new Pons launch goes through the Gate; only clean passes with real buying are posted to t.me/troyasentry.
  2. F2Trade in TelegramBuy and sell from a private chat with the bot through your Agent Wallet: the Gate runs first and every trade needs Confirm.
  3. F3Holder perksHold 1,000,000 $TROYA for 200 assistant messages a day, 100 playbooks and Sentry results by private message.
  4. F4Troya Gate badge OpenA live badge any project can put on its site, re-checked at most every 10 minutes. A snapshot, never a safe stamp.

Named for the prophet nobody believed. Eight phases that show their work: every call graded, every coin x-rayed.

  1. F13The Record OpenEvery Sentry call graded in public: the price at the call, an hour and a day later, and the peak.
  2. F14The Assay OpenDev wallets, holder clusters, bundled buys, wash trades and who funded the dev, under the Gate.
  3. F15TWAP and Smart DCA OpenBig orders cut into slices under a price-impact cap, and buys sized by the 7-day average.
  4. F16More perps Opentrade.xyz stock, index and metal perps, and Lighter on Robinhood Chain.
  5. F17Stock desk OpenEvery Robinhood stock token with dividends and splits, baskets in one go, loans on Morpho.
  6. F18Liquidity desk OpenUniswap v3 ranges set from the pool's own volatility, watched around the clock.
  7. F19$TROYA flywheel OpenCreator fees, buybacks, burns and locked liquidity, read back from the chain.
  8. F20Social layer OpenTroya on X, the Holder Pass, holder votes and an agent factory.

Odysseus

The long way home: tools for AI agents, and a signature on everything they touch.

  1. F21The chart oracle OpenEvery 6 hours a chart note for ETH, BTC, $TROYA and the top picks from fixed rules, graded in public 24 hours later.
  2. F22Troya CLI OpenThe desk in your terminal: npx @troyaagent/cli gate TROYA.
  3. F23Gate for every agent OpenFresh Gate checks at an agent's own trade size, signed by Troya, paid per check in USDG after the free ones.

Hermes

The messenger of the gods, and of trade. Troya moves into your X and Telegram. Each phase ships after a real-money test.

  1. F26Launch on X GuideTag @Troyaagent with a name, a $TICKER and an image, and the coin goes live on Pons from your Agent Wallet. Open to everyone: one from X a day, no limit with 1M $TROYA.
  2. F27Launch in TelegramBuilt and in its real-money test. Once it opens: the same from the bot, for everyone.
  3. F28Creator desk OpenEvery coin you launched through Troya, its creator fees read live from Pons, and a one-click claim signed by your own wallet.
  4. F29Trade on XBuilt and in its real-money test. Once it opens: “@Troyaagent buy 0.01 $CLAUS” or “sell 50% $CLAUS”, from your Agent Wallet, through the Gate and your own limits.
  5. F30Born at the gate OpenEvery coin launched through Troya shows its live Troya Gate verdict on Troya Launches and takes its place on The Record from its first block.
