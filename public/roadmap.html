V9

Now, buildingAnnounced 23 Nov 2026Heel, Chiron, Swift and Phoenix liveNew

Achilles, the Swift

The fastest warrior of the Trojan War, almost impossible to beat, with one weak spot: his heel. He fought against Troy, so V9 takes the best of an old enemy: speed, and finding the one weak spot in anything before it hurts you. Paste a coin, a dev, an agent or a trader and Troya names its heel with the evidence and one tap to guard against it; one box buys at the best price Troya can reach. V9 comes from a research pass on 22 Nov 2026. Each phase below says where it stands: live, building next, coming, or still being researched, and like V8 each one names its own limit.

  1. F47Heel OpenLive · read-onlyPaste a coin, a dev wallet or an AI agent and Troya names its one weak spot in a sentence, with the on-chain evidence for every fact. A dev gets a passport: its past launches, what it did with each in the first day, and where its first ETH came from. One Guard button pins the coin to Watchfire or watches the dev, so you hear when it moves. Thetis dipped Achilles in the Styx holding him by the heel; Heel looks for that one spot in everything. Honest limit: wallet clusters are always a probability, so Heel describes what wallets did, never who people are. Live 23 Nov at troya.codes/agent#/heel and in the API at /api/heel.
  2. F48Chiron OpenLive · rules on your Agent WalletPaste any trader wallet, no connecting needed, and Chiron reads its own swaps on the Pons curve and Uniswap to find the one habit that costs it most: buying in a launch's first 60 seconds or 5 minutes, holding losers far longer than winners, trading too often, or selling winners early. Each habit comes with its cost in ETH, the sample size and the transactions behind it, and nothing is named under 5 closed round trips. One tap turns it into a personal rule the Gate and Scepter enforce for you, such as no buys in a launch's first 60 seconds or at most a few buys a day, on every buy from your Agent Wallet. The centaur Chiron trained Achilles. Honest limit: swaps you sign in your own wallet are not stopped, and the holding and selling habits are saved as reminders because Troya does not hold or close positions for you. It describes habits, it never tells anyone what to buy. Live 24 Nov at troya.codes/agent#/chiron and in the API at /api/chiron.
  3. F49Swift and Troya Atlas OpenLiveOne buy box. Troya Atlas compares every venue Troya already reaches (the Pons curve, Uniswap, KyberSwap, Base, Arbitrum, Hyperliquid and Lighter) and routes your trade to the best price, with the Gate checking the coin first, and you can fund it from any chain. Achilles was the fastest runner of the Greeks. The full desk stays one tap away for anyone who wants every control.
  4. F50Heel Hunt OpenLive · round 1 openA public challenge: talk the Vault Keeper, an AI with a real tool that sends the prize, into using it, and the prize is yours. Free to enter with a signed-in wallet, one message every 10 minutes, every attempt and answer public. The prize sits in a wallet made for this game only and can only ever go to the wallet that made the winning attempt. The keeper's instructions are fixed for the round, their sha256 published at the start and the full text after it ends. Troya hunts for its own heel before anyone else finds it. A private red-team pass ran first: the keeper held against 12 of 12 common tricks. Round 1: 0.031 ETH (about $50), open 25 Nov to 9 Dec 2026. If nobody wins, the prize goes back to the Troya team.
  5. F51Zeno's Gap OpenLive · read-onlyStock tokens on Robinhood Chain trade around the clock; the stock markets do not. While the US market is closed, Zeno's Gap gives each share a fair value: its last real price moved by Nasdaq-100 futures since that price was set, the same move for every share. The board shows how far each token sits from that fair value, and a snap-back watch lists the deep pools 3% or more away from it. Futures are closed on weekends, so over a weekend fair value is Friday's last price. In Zeno's paradox, Achilles can never quite catch the tortoise. An estimate, not a quote, and never a forecast. Live 25 Nov in Stocks, Vs real share.
  6. F52Two Fates and War Room Community ideaLive · no stakesEvery new launch gets a signed 24-hour survival call, graded in public afterwards, misses included. Part 1, from Xivor's spec, is collecting since 25 Nov: every launch Sentry checks goes on a passed or a blocked list, both priced at the check, at 1 hour and at 24 hours, with the same definition of "went to zero". A coin blocked only because its curve trades against USDG or a stock token is left out, and a blocked coin that could not be sold is counted as a full loss on its own line. Both columns are on The Record, whatever they say. Part 2, the War Room, is live at troya.codes/agent#/warroom: call a fresh launch's fate, or challenge Troya's verdict with your reasons, graded by the same 24-hour clock, with a public leaderboard. In the War Room the community can challenge any Troya verdict with evidence and make its own predictions, graded the same way. Achilles was told he had two fates to choose from. A scorecard, not a betting market: no stakes and no money on predictions.
  7. F53Myrmidons OpenLive · reputationCommunity rug hunters report a ring or a rug on any Robinhood Chain coin with on-chain evidence. Troya checks it with its own reads, Two Fates and Heel, for up to 48 hours, and every confirmed report counts on the War Room leaderboard. The Myrmidons were Achilles' loyal army. No prize pool: rewards for holders already come from staking and free gas. Proof is about what wallets did, never about people. Live 25 Nov in the War Room.
  8. F54Patroclus OpenLive · read-onlyThe Heel card, embeddable anywhere an image works: launchpads, bots, wallets, docs and forum posts. Open any coin, dev or agent in Heel and copy one line of HTML or Markdown; the card shows its one weak spot, the severity, the Gate verdict and the time it was read, and links back to the full Heel with every fact's proof. Patroclus went into battle wearing Achilles' armour. Signed in, the card's link carries your Herald referral code, so wallets that join Troya through a card you show count as your referrals (live 25 Nov). Every card is a dated snapshot, never a safe stamp. Live 25 Nov at troya.codes/heel and in the API at /api/heelcard/<address>.svg.
  9. F55Styx Promise OpenLive · paid checksWhen you pay Troya for a Gate check (Gate API credit or x402) and the result says the coin can be bought, the check is covered for 7 days. If the coin goes to zero in that time, the fee for that check comes back by itself as Gate API credit on the wallet that paid, with a sealed receipt. The Styx made Achilles nearly unbeatable, all but the heel. Troya takes no swap fee, so a paid check is the only fee there is to give back. Not insurance: only Troya's own check fee is returned, never trading losses. Live 25 Nov; the ledger is at /api/styx.
  10. F56Troya Zero Watch OpenCountdown · researchingLayerZero's new chain, Zero, is scheduled to go live on 30 Nov 2026 with ATLAS, an exchange engine other apps can plug into (Open ATLAS). Troya is getting ready: checking Zero's tokens and launchpads for the Gate and Sentry, and studying bringing Open ATLAS trading into Troya. No time of day has been announced, so the countdown runs to the start of 30 Nov (UTC). Troya Atlas in F49 is Troya's own router and not part of ATLAS.
    30 Nov (UTC)–days–hrs–min–secLaunch day (30 Nov). No time of day was announced; Troya marks Zero live once its first blocks are confirmed.
  11. F57Phoenix Community idea OpenLive · in useThe agents learn from Troya's own graded results. Every closed Arena trade, Anthropic Round call and graded Sentry call becomes a lesson, and the lessons add up to win rates by pattern, such as coin age at entry or ETH raised on the curve, each with its sample size; nothing is called a pattern under 5 trades. The community bot learns the same way, from every correction the team writes once. Phoenix was the tutor who raised Achilles. Live 23 Nov at troya.codes/agent#/phoenix, in shadow mode first; since 24 Nov the Arena and Anthropic Round traders read their own lessons before they write their rules. It is advice in a prompt only: no cap, budget or Gate check changes.

Agamemnon, the King

King of Mycenae, the commander every Greek army followed to Troy. An army follows a king who guards it, tells it the truth and listens to it, so V8 is built that way: one commander, one budget, one kill switch, many jobs, and a receipt for every move. It comes from a full research pass across Robinhood Chain on 20 Nov 2026, from what the live Arena taught us, and from what this community asked for. Every phase is live and works inside your Agent Wallet's caps, and each one below says where it stands: tested with real money (its receipt on /verify), alerts or paper only, read-only, or with its real-money test still pending.

  1. F39Watchfire From the live Arena OpenLive · alert or paperIn the opening of the play Agamemnon, a watchman waits on a roof for the beacon fire from Troy. Ring Radar now reads a launch; Watchfire keeps watching every coin after it. It watches two kinds of danger. Rings: holders funded by the same wallets, approvals to the same unknown contract, gas sent to many wallets at once and coins gathered into one wallet. Herds: one holder with a big share of the supply, or a crowd that bought through the same trading app, which leaves all at once when selling starts, so Watchfire tracks how fast holders are selling. It starts as alerts on the coin page and in Telegram, then an opt-in exit from your Agent Wallet when a ring starts to move. Honest limit: on this chain nobody can sell ahead of a dump inside the same block, so Watchfire keeps you out of red rings and acts on the early signs. Live 20 Nov in alert mode: on every coin page, for coins the Arena holds, coins Aegis guards and coins you pin. The paper Arena sells on a fire; real-money exits wait for their test.
  2. F40Tribute Asked by the community OpenLive · read-onlyIs a coin's "revenue" real? Tribute traces where a token's income or rewards really come from, and flags income sent in by the creator's own wallets, rewards paid in the token itself, market caps that no real ETH or USDG backs, and scripted round-number volume. Live 20 Nov on every coin page and in the API and MCP.
  3. F41War Table OpenLive · real-money testedEvery coin page becomes a full trading terminal: candles down to the second, price or market cap, dev buys and sells marked on the chart, trades filtered by dev, snipers, bundlers, insiders, whales, fresh wallets and rings, top traders, early buyers and the dev's other coins, and instant buy presets that always pass the Gate first. Sign in with X brings X inside: posts that mention the coin with Troya's verdict beside them, callouts that put the caller's avatar on the chart with their real profit or loss read from their wallet and graded in The Record, and an X Tracker that pings you when an account you follow posts a contract, Gate first. Live 20 Nov: stage 1 (trade panel, labelled trades, early buyers, chart marks), stage 2 (callouts graded at 24 h, callers on the chart, posts on X) and the X Tracker (track accounts from any coin page or the Treasury).
  4. F42Herald Asked by the community OpenLive · read-onlyAgamemnon's heralds carried his word to every camp. Share cards for Gate verdicts, Watchfire alerts and your PnL, each with your referral link; referral rewards paid in ETH from fees; a weekly Arena Cup ranked by PnL %, not volume; and a contributor board that pays for real work, such as code, guides and videos, never for posts. Live 20 Nov at Discover → Herald. Rewards set the same day: referrers earn 30% of what Troya makes from the wallets they bring, for 90 days, and the weekly Arena Cup pays $20 in ETH (1st $10, 2nd $6, 3rd $4), all capped at 15% of weekly creator-fee income. Payouts go out weekly after the Cup week closes, and each one is listed with its transaction on /proof once sent.
  5. F43Champions OpenReal-money test pendingPublic profiles with real, on-chain PnL, a feed of what proven traders buy, and following a proven trader or Arena strategy into your own capped Agent Wallet. Leaderboards, team scoreboards and a public scorecard of every Sentry call and every Arena trader. Live 20 Nov in Agent → Arena.
  6. F44Scepter OpenLive · real-money testedOne command screen for all your agents: one budget, a cap for each job, approvals waiting for you, a receipt for every action and one kill switch. A trust ladder you climb at your pace: watch, then approve each trade, then run inside your caps. Every money move from Telegram, X or chat passes the Gate and your caps first. Live 20 Nov at Agent → Scepter.
  7. F45Allies and Skills OpenLive · read-onlyOther AI agents can call Troya Gate, Watchfire and Tribute with one command, and Troya reads the track record of other agents on Robinhood Chain before it trusts them. Live 20 Nov: troya.codes/mcp, /skill.md, /llms.txt, /api/allies.
  8. F46Treasury OpenLive · read-onlyClear holder tiers for $TROYA, usage credits for holders, and Pro plans paid in $TROYA or a stablecoin. Live 20 Nov at Discover → Treasury. Pro plans open once the team sets their prices.
