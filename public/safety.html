01 / Never

Five things Troya never does

If anyone claiming to be Troya does one of these, it is not Troya. Report it in the Community page or the Telegram group.

01

Ask for your seed phrase or key

Not in the app, not on X, not in Telegram, not ever. Nothing Troya does needs it.

02

DM you first

The team never opens a private chat with you. The Troya bot writes to you only after you link it yourself in the app, for your alerts. Anyone else who DMs you as Troya is an impostor.

03

Sign for your wallet

Every trade from your own wallet is signed in that wallet, by you. Troya prepares it and shows you the check; it cannot send it.

04

Send your Agent Wallet anywhere else

A withdrawal from your Agent Wallet can only go to your own connected wallet. There is no other address it can be sent to.

05

Touch your creator fee

On a coin launched through Troya, your share of the fee is fixed in a contract when the coin launches. Nobody, Troya included, can change it.

02 / Your money

Where your money sits

Three places, three rules. The full detail, keys for Hyperliquid and Lighter included, is on Risk Controls.

Your wallet

Held by you
  • Troya never sees or stores its key.
  • Every swap, launch and claim waits for your signature in the wallet itself.
  • Disconnect at any time; nothing keeps running in its name.

Agent Wallet

Optional pocket money
  • Used for launching and trading from X and Telegram; it holds only what you send it.
  • Acts only inside your own caps, and not at all while you pause it.
  • Withdraws only to your own wallet. Export its key whenever you like.
  • Kept encrypted on Troya's server, so keep in it only what you would carry in a pocket.

Creator fees

Yours, by contract
  • Hold 1,000,000 $TROYA: the whole creator fee goes straight to your wallet.
  • Otherwise a fee-split contract pays you 0.6% of volume and Troya 0.1%. Your share and your address are fixed when the coin launches.
  • The contract has no function to move your part or point the fees anywhere else. Troya's share can never be set above 20% of a creator's fees.
  • Claim in one click on the Creator desk; anyone may trigger the payout, and it always pays you.
