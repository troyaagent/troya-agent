Proof · Seal

Receipts, not promises

Troya seals what it decides in public. Today that is every Gate API check (paid over x402 and Virtuals ACP too), Council verdict, red Aegis alert, Arena strategy and trade, and Sentry call on The Record, plus Ring Radar flags, Watchfire fires, Oaths taken and broken, War Table callouts, Owl journals and the public randomness draws. A receipt is signed twice, by the Troya attestor (ECDSA) and with a post-quantum ML-DSA-65 key, and chained to the one before it. Once a day the chain is anchored on Robinhood Chain, so a closed day cannot be rewritten, by Troya or anyone else.

02 / Proven contracts

16 properties, proven for every input

Troya's three contracts on Robinhood Chain, TroyaFeeSplitterFactory 0x2663…F525, TroyaPass 0x439E…a77E and TroyaOathVault 0x6Bb9…a4e8, were checked with Halmos, a16z's symbolic prover. A fuzz test tries random values; Halmos proves each property for all of them. Run it yourself: uvx --python 3.12 halmos --match-path 'test/halmos/*' in the repo's contracts folder. These are proofs of the listed properties, not a claim that the code has no other bugs.

01 · TroyaFeeSplitter

Every split adds up

For any amount and any allowed share, the treasury gets exactly amount × share ÷ 10,000, the creator gets the rest, and nothing stays behind.

check_splitAddsUpExactly

02 · TroyaFeeSplitter

Troya's share is capped at 20%

No splitter can ever be created with a share above 2,000 basis points.

check_shareCappedAt20Percent

03 · TroyaFeeSplitter

Set once, never changed

Nobody, Troya included, can re-initialize a splitter to a new creator or share.

check_cannotReinitialize

04 · TroyaFeeSplitter

The owner cannot touch the creator's part

Changing where Troya's share goes never changes the creator, the share, or what the creator receives.

check_ownerCannotTouchCreatorsPart

05 · TroyaFeeSplitter

Only the owner moves Troya's share

Any other caller trying to change the treasury fails, and nothing changes.

check_onlyOwnerSetsTreasury

06 · TroyaFeeSplitter

Money kept for a creator stays theirs

If a creator cannot receive ETH, the amount is kept for them. Only they can withdraw it, and it is never split again.

check_owedOnlyToItsOwner

07 · TroyaPass

Only holders can mint

A pass mints only for a wallet holding at least 1,000,000 $TROYA, and always to that wallet.

check_mintNeedsTheMinimum

08 · TroyaPass

One pass per wallet

A second mint always fails, whatever the balance.

check_onePassPerWallet

09 · TroyaPass

Soulbound

No transfer path moves a pass: not by its holder, not by anyone else, to any address.

check_cannotBeTransferred

10 · TroyaPass

No approvals

approve and setApprovalForAll always fail, so nobody can ever be allowed to move a pass.

check_noApprovals

11 · TroyaPass

Only the holder burns

Anyone else's burn changes nothing; the holder's burn removes the pass.

check_onlyHolderBurns

12 · TroyaOathVault

Nothing leaves before the cliff

Before a lock's cliff, every withdrawal fails, whatever amount is asked.

check_nothingBeforeCliff

13 · TroyaOathVault

Never faster than the rate

A withdrawal never takes more than the daily rate has unlocked by then, so a dev can never sell faster than its Oath allows.

check_neverFasterThanTheRate

14 · TroyaOathVault

Only the beneficiary

Only the wallet the coins are locked for can take them out or change the terms. The vault has no owner and no admin.

check_onlyBeneficiary

15 · TroyaOathVault

Terms only get stricter

After any change, the cliff is no earlier and the daily rate no higher than before.

check_termsOnlyTighten

16 · TroyaOathVault

Never more than was locked

Everything taken out of a lock, over two withdrawals at any times, stays within what was locked, and the vault always holds exactly the rest.

check_totalWithinLock Real-money test, step 32

03 / Quantum-Ready

Signed for the next era too

ECDSA keys like every Ethereum wallet's are expected to be breakable by a large enough quantum computer one day. Each receipt therefore also carries an ML-DSA-65 signature (NIST FIPS 204), a post-quantum scheme, so the record stays checkable either way. Quantum-Ready, not quantum-proof: Robinhood Chain itself, like every EVM chain, still runs on ECDSA.
