Safety · Gate in your wallet

Troya Gate, on your confirm screen

The Troya Gate Snap adds a tab to MetaMask's confirm screen. Before you sign anything that touches a coin on Robinhood Chain, it shows the Gate's verdict for that coin: the buy and a test sell simulated, taxes, pool depth, honeypot and blacklist patterns, owner powers. It also warns you about unlimited approvals. It only reads the transaction you are about to sign. It never asks for keys and cannot sign or send anything.

01 / Install

One click in MetaMask

Today it runs in MetaMask Flask, MetaMask's developer build. Regular MetaMask follows once MetaMask's allowlist review is done. The code is public in GitHub and the package is @troyaagent/snap on npm.

02 / What it reads

Only what you are about to sign

For each transaction, the Snap sends the sending address, the target contract, the calldata, the ETH value and the chain to Troya Gate at troya.codes, and shows the answer. Your balances, keys and seed phrase stay in MetaMask. The Snap's answers are live snapshots, not receipts; every Gate API check, Council verdict, red Aegis alert, Arena trade and Record call is signed and listed on Seal, and the Gate itself is tested with real money on Verify.

03 / Questions

Before you install

Short answers about the Troya Gate Snap. Anything else: ask in the Troya community or send a report at troya.codes/community.

FUNDS

Can it touch my money?

No. It only reads the transaction you are about to sign and shows Troya Gate's verdict. It has no access to your keys or seed phrase, and it cannot sign, send or approve anything. You always decide with MetaMask's own Confirm or Cancel.

VERDICT

What do the verdicts mean?

Passed: the buy and a test sell simulate fine and nothing in taxes, depth or owner powers stands out. Caution: something deserves a look, with the reason shown. Failed: a check failed, such as a sell that would not go through; MetaMask shows it as critical. It is a snapshot from Troya Gate, never a safe stamp.

CHAINS

Which transactions does it check?

Transactions on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663) that touch a coin: swaps on any app, buys on a Pons curve, approvals. On other chains it stays quiet. Unlimited approvals get a warning on their own.

FLASK

Why MetaMask Flask for now?

MetaMask reviews every Snap that uses internet access before regular MetaMask can install it. Troya Gate has been submitted to the MetaMask Snaps Directory; until it is listed, it runs in Flask, MetaMask's developer build.

DATA

What is sent to Troya?

The sending address, the target contract, the calldata, the ETH value and the chain of the transaction you are about to sign, to get the verdict. Not your balances, not your keys, nothing else from your wallet.

REMOVE

How do I remove it?

In MetaMask: menu, Snaps, Troya Gate, Remove. It takes effect at once and leaves nothing behind.
