New to Troya? Start here

Troya checks a coin before you buy it and keeps a public record of how its checks held up. It works on Robinhood Chain, with swaps on Base, Arbitrum and Solana and perps on Hyperliquid. It never holds your main wallet: you sign your own trades, or use an Agent Wallet with caps you set.

01 / Three things to try first

Three things to try first

  1. Check a coin. Paste any Robinhood Chain contract at troya.codes/check, or send /check 0x… to the Troya bot in Telegram. You get one score out of 100 with the reasons behind it. Free, nothing to sign.
  2. Run the Gate before a buy. Gate a coin simulates your exact buy and a test sell, and reads fees, taxes and liquidity. Swap runs it again every time you press Confirm.
  3. See if the checks were right. The Record grades every call in public, and Two Fates compares the coins the Gate passed with the ones it blocked, at 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days.

Everything Troya claims can be checked without a wallet on /proof. Automated checks, never an audit and never a safe stamp. Questions: the Telegram group t.me/troyaagent, or team@troya.codes in private.
