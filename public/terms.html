Who we are and what this covers

What the service is

Troya reads market data and prepares orders on Robinhood Chain (chain 4663), Base, Arbitrum, Solana and Hyperliquid (perps, spot and the trade.xyz stock perps), and bridges ETH to and from Arbitrum, Base and Ethereum. On Robinhood Chain it also offers Lighter perps, loans against tokenized stocks on Morpho Blue and Uniswap v3 liquidity ranges, and it lets anyone launch a coin on Pons Family, on troya.codes or by posting on X (Launch in Telegram is built but not open yet). Troya also answers on X and in Telegram. You can approve and sign each action in your own wallet, or you can turn on an optional Agent Wallet that acts for you inside limits you set.

The service is a beta running on mainnet. Transactions move real digital assets, cannot be reversed once confirmed, and can result in total loss.

The optional Agent Wallet

The Agent Wallet is custodial: the operator stores its private key in encrypted form so the agent can sign within your limits without asking each time. By turning it on you accept that:

  • it should only hold small amounts you can afford to lose;
  • it acts within the caps, leverage limit and allowlist you configure, and the operator may set stricter global caps;

  • you may export its key at any time, after which you are responsible for keeping it safe;
  • despite our safeguards, custodial keys carry risks such as service compromise or outage.

Launching a coin

When you launch a coin through Troya, you are its creator and you alone are responsible for its name, ticker, image and anything said to promote it. A launched coin lives on the chain for good; neither you nor Troya can take it back.

  • Troya charges nothing to launch. You pay the Pons launch fee (0.0005 ETH today) and, when you sign on troya.codes, the gas. For a launch from X your Agent Wallet pays the Pons fee and Troya pays the gas.
  • You may set a creator tax from 0 to 4%; it is paid to you.
  • Without 1,000,000 $TROYA in your wallet and its Agent Wallet, you can launch 3 coins a day (UTC), at most 1 of them from X. The coin's creator fee then goes to a fee-split contract whose split nobody can change, which pays you 0.6% of trading volume plus the creator tax and pays Troya 0.1% of volume. Traders pay nothing extra.
  • With 1,000,000 $TROYA or more there is no daily limit and the whole creator fee (0.7% of volume plus the creator tax) goes to your own wallet.
  • Do not launch a coin that impersonates a person, project or brand, or that is meant to mislead buyers. Troya refuses tickers already used by major coins and stock tokens, and names that contain Troya, official, admin, team, support or Robinhood.

Limits reduce risk; they do not remove it. A limit that is set too high, a price that moves fast, or a venue that behaves unexpectedly can still cause losses.
