06

Troya Sentry

Every new Pons launch is checked by Troya Gate about 90 seconds in, and again at 5, 15 and 60 minutes while it is quiet. New Uniswap v3 and v4 pools and new pools on the other Robinhood Chain launchpads go through the same checks. The public channel posts the clean passes with real buying: at least 0.5 ETH on the curve (or in the pool) and 25 or more independent buyers. Wallets that buy every new coin, such as bots and farms, are not counted. Names with look-alike letters are left out, and so is a coin reusing a ticker posted in the last 24 hours, unless it has more than twice the independent buyers of the first one (that post then names the other coin). Every result, failed and caution included, is in the JSON.

Channel, readable without Telegram Latest results (JSON)

07

The Record

Every coin Troya Sentry posts is a call: its price is written down at the post and graded in public 24 hours later, a win if it is higher, a miss if not. Nothing is deleted, misses included.
