06

Troya Sentry

Every new Pons launch is checked by Troya Gate about 90 seconds in, and again at 5, 15 and 60 minutes while it is quiet. New Uniswap v3 and v4 pools and new pools on the other Robinhood Chain launchpads go through the same checks. The public channel posts the clean passes with real buying: at least 0.5 ETH on the curve (or in the pool) and 25 or more independent buyers. Wallets that buy every new coin, such as bots and farms, are not counted, and copies of a ticker already posted that day or names with look-alike letters are left out. Every result, failed and caution included, is in the JSON.
