02 / What Zero and ATLAS are

In plain words

  • Zero is a new blockchain made by LayerZero. Its work is split into zones, for EVM apps, for trading and for private payments, and it uses succinct proofs so a computation can be checked without running it again (LayerZero lists zero-knowledge proofs as a later step).
  • ATLAS is an exchange engine on Zero that matches, clears and settles trades in one system. It has no app of its own: trading venues put their own screens on top of it.
  • Open ATLAS is the standard setup for crypto trading apps: a venue connects, picks or creates markets and serves its users without building an exchange engine.
  • Zero and ATLAS are scheduled to launch on 30 Nov 2026. That date is LayerZero's; no time of day has been announced, so this countdown runs to the start of 30 Nov in UTC.

Sources: Introducing ATLAS (LayerZero blog, 25 Aug 2026) and Stake ZRO on Zero (LayerZero blog, 20 Nov 2026, launch date).
